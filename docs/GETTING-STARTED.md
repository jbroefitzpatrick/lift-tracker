# Getting started

No downloads, accounts or app store needed. The app is a website you save to your phone's home screen, where it works like a normal app.

## The link

**https://jbroefitzpatrick.github.io/lift-tracker/**

That's the whole app. Send this link to anyone who wants to use it.

## Put it on your phone

### iPhone

1. Open the link in **Safari** (the blue compass app). Other browsers on iPhone can't add apps to the home screen.
2. Tap the **Share** button at the bottom: a square with an arrow pointing up.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. Open **Lift** from your home screen.

### Android

1. Open the link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu (three dots, top right).
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**, then confirm.
4. Open **Lift** from your home screen.

Always open it from the home-screen icon, not from the browser. That keeps your data safer and lets it work without signal.

## First time you open it

- Tap **Build my plan** and answer a few questions: goals, experience, how many days, how long. Or tap **Use sample plan** to look around first.
- Most things explain themselves. Tap any exercise for its details, how-to and history.
- The **⋯** button at the top of the Train tab has the day options: plan this week, adjust today, report pain, intervals and recovery routines.

## Your data

- Everything you log stays **on your phone**. Nobody else can see it, including the person who sent you the link.
- To protect it, go to **Settings (gear icon) → Data & security**:
  - **Cloud backup**: automatic and encrypted. You'll need the access key from whoever runs the beta. Save the recovery code it shows you.
  - **Back up now**: saves a file to your phone or iCloud Drive.

## Updates

Updates arrive automatically. Close the app and open it again, and you'll have the newest version within a launch or two.

## Sharing it

In the app: **Settings → Share or install the app**. That screen has the link and these same steps.

---

### For the person running the beta

You don't need to install or run anything.

- **Hosting:** the app is hosted free by GitHub Pages from the `lift-tracker` repository. Every change pushed to the `main` branch goes live at the link above within a minute or two.
- **Cloud backup server:** this is already running (see [CLOUD-BACKUP.md](CLOUD-BACKUP.md)). Testers need the access key to turn it on.
- **Ignore the code folders:** the `js`, `cloud`, `docs` and `tools` folders are only for making changes to the app. Nobody needs to open them to use it.
